//.ABOUT ➤ BIO.

I am an artist who specializes in 3D environment art for video-games and interactive platforms. Evolving from the small-scale hobbyist modeling of my middle school days to organized environment design, my work is inspired by futurism, fantasy, and the escapism of first-person shooter titles. Shaped by the ever changing world of 3D graphics, I bring otherworldly concepts to life within the spaces I’ve built, using software both old and new. Through this, I seek to spark people’s imagination, ushering them to pursue 3D arts with the same fascination as I do.

//.ABOUT ➤ CAPTURING THE IMPOSSIBLE.

I am an environment artist who specializes in creating assets and virtual environments for interactive 3D platforms and video-games. I have worked with Blender, Maya, Substance Painter, Photoshop/Adobe Suite, HDRshop, and Marmoset, and am familiar with workflows compatible with Unity, UE5, and Source.


My work addresses visions of “what could be”, using themes of futurism and mystique. These involve overarching concepts such as industrial cityscapes, space stations, and alien life; they play into the strange and distant, while also following familiar throughlines of spaces that feel lived in.Simulated space allows for the exploration of settings and concepts that would be otherwise impossible to experience within daily life.


Fundamentally, these environments are designed to invoke a state of presence in the viewer. Both subtle and overt interactive elements are implemented into the navigable space, enabling the ability to influence the viewers’ actions and perspective. This forms a direct engagement between the viewer and the work, resulting in a more personal, memorable experience. Through this medium, I find fulfillment in creating immersive work that acknowledges its viewers by using interactability, dynamic functionality, and natural curiosity.
